How it works

  1. Drop a pinAny coordinates in Armenia.
  2. Pull the dataClimate, land cover, soil and land value from free public APIs.

Climate data: PVGIS (EU Joint Research Centre) typical year for Yerevan. Training data: US EIA plant generation, NASA POWER.

Prices, costs and dust loss are editable starting assumptions, not quotes. Panel heat estimate: cell temperature from air temperature and sunlight, 0.4% output loss per °C above 25 °C. Yield assumes optimally tilted fixed panels (+13% sunlight vs. flat) and 10% equipment losses.
